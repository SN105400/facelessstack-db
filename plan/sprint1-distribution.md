## Dead or dangerous

- **Thin programmatic pages** (location/definition/boilerplate) — Google's scaled content abuse policy + AI Overviews killed it (40–60% traffic losses reported).
- **Etsy mass AI listings** — banned; requires "meaningful human involvement"; printables saturated.
- **ThemeForest/Envato** — sales collapsing (>50% down), Elements cannibalized one-time sales, exclusive author program ended with flat 50% commission (July 2026).
- **Parasite SEO** — site reputation abuse manual actions since May 2024; hardened Nov 2024.
- **Generic AI content blogs as the income engine** — zero-click reality (58%+ of searches), scaled content abuse risk.
- **Gumroad Discover as a traffic source** — zero organic discovery; checkout layer only.
- **Astroturfed Reddit/X accounts** — bans destroy the one channel Google still boosts.
- **Mobile app stores as primary channel** — 78% of Play apps launched since early 2025 have <1,000 downloads; paid dev fees.

## Persona verdict

**Best strategy: a "software-artifact marketplace factory + citation-bait data layer."** Spend nightly tokens on things marketplaces index natively and don't punish for volume: Chrome extensions, VS Code/OpenVSX extensions, MCP servers, Raycast/Figma plugins, Framer templates — each an installment of one theme (e.g., developer productivity), each linking to a shared free tool site, monetized freemium via Gumroad/ExtensionPay. Use leftover tokens to generate and nightly-refresh genuinely unique datasets/calculators on that site — pages built to be *cited by* AI Overviews and ChatGPT (GEO), not to win blue links. One human morning pass ships ~5 vetted artifacts/week.

**Biggest mistake to avoid: burning 300M tokens/night on mass-produced SEO content pages.** Scale is actively penalized (scaled content abuse), the traffic has collapsed (65% CTR drop on AIO queries, ~83% zero-click), and it converts at ~$0. Tokens should build *indexable software and data*, not indexable prose.
