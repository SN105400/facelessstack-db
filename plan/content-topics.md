# Content Topic Research — Facebook Reels (2026-11-19)

What actually earns views on Facebook, matched against what our text→code→animation pipeline can produce. Pipeline constraint: **no photoreal humans/animals/scenes** (grant is text-only; we generate graphics, typography, and abstract motion as code).

## What the research says (2025–2026)

- **Entertainment is the #1 views niche** on Facebook; short punchy storytelling clips outperform static quotes.
- **Highest CPM (revenue per view):** finance & investing → real estate → technology/AI → business. Health/wellness, motivation, education also strong.
- Faceless pages in cooking/homesteading/reselling/motherhood/lifestyle niches report $80–105K/mo at the extreme top (survivor-biased — treat as ceiling, not expectation).
- Facebook's unified **Content Monetization** program pays Reels; reach levers in 2026: search-optimized captions, Groups distribution, and the older demographic Facebook holds that Instagram doesn't.
- Consistency in ONE niche beats going broad — a single viral reel cascades reach onto the page's older posts.
- Sources: [publisherinabox.com/most-profitable-facebook-niches](https://publisherinabox.com/most-profitable-facebook-niches) and creator-reported data (r/AskMarketing threads).

## Rules (from the compliance playbook + FB reality)
1. Original generative art only — never repost/compile others' content (inauthentic-content trap).
2. AI-generated label ON (Meta requires disclosure; originality is what keeps monetization safe).
3. One niche identity per page until metrics say otherwise; caption search-optimized; post to relevant Groups where allowed.
4. Expectation-setting: monetization thresholds (followers + watch-minutes) = months of consistent posting; treat months 1–2 as reach experiments, measured weekly.

## Genre decision framework (2026-11-19, user asked: political / trivial / what-if / history?)

Policy anchor: Meta's Content Monetization Policies put **controversial political/social content in restricted inventory = little/no payout**; faceless operators must run a **Page or professional-mode profile** (personal profiles are ineligible). Source: facebook.com/business/help/1348682518563619.

| Genre | Monetization | Views fit | Token fit | Asset synergy | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Political** | ❌ restricted inventory | divisive spikes, unstable | cheap | none | **NEVER on the money track** |
| **Trivial / everyday comedy** | ✅ safe | ✅ top views niche | cheap | weak-direct | **Page-1 core** (stickman skits: work, gym, family, "POV" formats) |
| **What-if** (science/tech/counterfactual) | ✅ safe if de-politicized | evergreen, high shares | **high — research-heavy** | medium | **Page-2 experiment** |
| **History** ("on this day", forgotten stories) | ✅ safe (educational framing) | older FB demo loves it | **high — fact-check burden** | medium | **Page-2 experiment** |

Rules: one lane per page (consistency compounds reach); fleet drafts 10 skits/night → user curates 2–3 at the morning gate; history content gets a mandatory fact-check pass (hallucinated history = reputation death); what-if frames science/tech/economics, never contested modern politics. AI/tech satire sits safely between comedy and the asset — vendors are an industry, not an electorate.
