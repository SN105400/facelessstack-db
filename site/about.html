Methodology

Data is collected from public, logged-out pricing pages at most once per day per provider (rate-limited, robots.txt respected). Every row stores its source URL and fetch timestamp. Prices are reproduced as displayed — currency USD unless stated. This site is independent and unaffiliated with any listed vendor; outbound links may be affiliate links. Corrections: open an issue on the repository.
